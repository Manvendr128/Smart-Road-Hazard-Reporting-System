ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Making Roads Safer for Everyone

Our mission is to make road hazard reporting easier, faster, and more effective through technology.

Who We Are

Smart Road Hazard System is a technology-based project designed to help people report road problems such as potholes, damaged roads, and other road hazards. It aims to improve road safety by making it easier to share hazard information.

The system allows users to capture or upload photos of road hazards and share their location along with the report. This information can help the concerned authorities identify road problems and take appropriate action.

Our Mission

To contribute towards safer roads by encouraging people to report road hazards and helping make road maintenance more efficient.

What We Aim For

Simple technology for a safer road experience.
