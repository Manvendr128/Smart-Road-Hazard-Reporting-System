How It Works

Three simple steps to report a road hazard.

01

Identify the Hazard

Notice a pothole, damaged road or another condition that may cause an accident.

02

Provide Details

Add a photograph, location and a short description of the road problem.

03

Submit the Report

Send the information through the system so the reported issue can be reviewed.
