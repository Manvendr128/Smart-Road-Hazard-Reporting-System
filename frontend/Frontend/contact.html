Civic Support & Helplines

The Smart Road Hazard System connects citizens directly with municipal road maintenance crews. If you need assistance with an urgent road blockage, severe crater, or flooding hazard, you can contact the responsible department through our official channels.

General Inquiries: info@smartroadhazard.com
Emergency Road Safety Helpline: 1800-ROADS-SAFE
Municipal Control Room: Central Municipal Headquarters, Road Infrastructure Cell

Fast Resolution

Citizen hazard reports submitted through our web portal are processed in real-time and queued for field engineering inspection.
